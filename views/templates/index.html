                Notes
                
          FiniSpeak SignNet v1
          
                
          An AI-powered, bidirectional sign-language system for FiniSpeak video calls. SignNet is meant to use the real-time translation engine, live subtitles, and the customizable avatar platform.
          
                
            
                    
          • Sign to speech: signs from the call video become captions with the live subtitles.
            • 
                    
          • Speech to sign: spoken words drive the call avatar so signing appears in the video tile.
